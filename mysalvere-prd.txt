The Story Behind Salvere 
Salvere was founded from a place of observation, experience, and a desire to do things 
differently. 
As a pharmacist, our founder worked closely with individuals living with chronic health 
conditions — dispensing medications and seeing, firsthand, the realities that came with them. 
Over time, something became difficult to ignore. Many people were not just managing their 
conditions — they were also managing the side effects of long-term medication. And for many, 
the idea of depending on these medications indefinitely, with little focus on underlying causes, 
raised an important question: Could more be done earlier? 
This question led to a deeper exploration of lifestyle, nutrition, and the body's internal 
environment. 
A Personal Journey 
Before her own body showed any signs of chronic imbalance, she began to apply what she was 
learning. She focused on supporting her body through nutrition, maintaining internal balance 
through daily habits, and building a lifestyle that prioritized long-term health. 
This approach became even more evident during pregnancy. Without relying on conventional 
prenatal supplements, which she could not tolerate, she focused on high-quality, nutrient-dense 
foods and a supportive lifestyle. Her experience was steady and uncomplicated. She maintained 
her health throughout pregnancy, delivered safely, and recovered well — eventually raising two 
healthy boys. For her, this was not just theory. It was lived experience. 
From Practice to Purpose 
What started as personal application became something more. Over the years, she began 
helping others adopt similar principles — guiding individuals and families toward better health 
through practical, everyday changes. Through education, consultations, and content, she has 
reached and supported hundreds — and indirectly, thousands — of people seeking a more 
structured approach to their health. In 2020, her work evolved further with formal training in 
functional medicine, deepening her ability to connect symptoms, lab results, and lifestyle 
patterns into clear, actionable plans. 
The Salvere Approach 
At the core of Salvere is a simple idea: Health improves when you understand what is driving it. 
We connect: 
• Lab results 
• Lifestyle and daily habits 
• Nutrition and hydration 
• Patterns within the body 
To help you understand: 
• What is happening 
• Why it is happening 
• What to do next 
Working Alongside Your Care 
Salvere does not replace medical care. We work alongside it — helping you understand your 
results more clearly, prepare for better conversations with your doctor, and take practical steps 
that support your overall health. 
Why Salvere 
Salvere is derived from Latin, meaning to be in good health. This platform was created to bring a 
more practical, contextual, and accessible approach to health — especially within a Nigerian 
setting. One that speaks your language. One that considers your reality. One that gives you 
something you can actually follow. 
Who This Is For 
Salvere is for individuals who: 
• Have lab results or diagnoses but want deeper understanding 
• Want a structured, thoughtful approach to their health 
• Prefer clarity over guesswork 
• Are ready to take practical steps toward improvement
