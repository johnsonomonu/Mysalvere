SECTION 4 — THE SALVERE APPROACH
At Salvere, we focus on restoring your system — not just managing symptoms.
For Dewumi Ebuk, this mission is deeply personal. After working in both clinical and corporate environments, I began to notice a pattern. Brilliant, hardworking people were constantly exhausted, and always on one stimulant or the other. Not because they lacked discipline, or didn’t know right from wrong, but because their bodies were overwhelmed and burnt-out
